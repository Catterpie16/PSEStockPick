1. Evolution of the Target Variable

Initially, we attempted to predict long-term horizons (60+ days). However, this introduced massive noise and confused the algorithms. We pivoted to a much cleaner, strict quantitative question:

"Will this stock gain at least 10% within the next 20 trading days?"

Rationale: A 10% gain in a month represents a highly profitable "swing trade," perfectly suited for blue-chip stocks on the PSEi.

2. Market Regime & Run-Time Optimization

More data is not always better. Feeding the model 10 years of historical data forced it to learn from the 2018 bull market peak and the extreme volatility of the 2020 COVID crash. This "Concept Drift" degraded our accuracy.

  • The Fix: We sliced the dataset to focus exclusively on the recent 5-year market regime (Mid-2021 to Present).
  • The Result: By isolating the current "sideways" market behavior, the model learned highly relevant patterns. Reducing dataset size also cut our computational run time by more than half.

3. Feature Engineering

Raw price data is highly noisy. We engineered 10 highly specific features, dropping underperforming ones like the traditional RSI. Core features include:

Trend Extension Dist_SMA50 & Dist_SMA200 measures how stretched a stock is from its moving averages.
Institutional Flow Foreign_Intensity tracks institutional money versus retail noise.

1. Handling the Math: Class Imbalance (9.70)

A 10% gain in 20 days is a rare event for Philippine mega-caps—it happens roughly 1 out of every 10 times. To prevent the model from simply guessing "0" to achieve artificial accuracy, we calculated an exact class imbalance ratio of 9.70 and applied algorithmic penalties (scale_pos_weight). This forced the models to actively hunt for true breakouts.
